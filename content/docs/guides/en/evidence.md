## What a result carries

A canonical assertion links subject, predicate, entity/literal object and source evidence to document, revision, chunk, mutation and pipeline identities. Bounded graph proof also preserves supporting assertion IDs, entities, predicates and directions. A path must stay attached to its own supporting sources.

## Three hashes have different meanings

A declared whole-revision content hash, frozen chunk manifest hash and per-chunk content hash identify different things. Direct chunk insertion does not establish the whole-revision hash. Typed legal identity checks structural linkage and bounded aliases; source interpretation remains the producer responsibility.

## Pack complete proof within a budget

ContextBuilder counts formatted model-visible evidence, not the entire HTTP response. Complete graph proof must fit atomically; if canonical BPE cannot load, the conservative UTF-8 byte upper bound may pack less evidence. Empty retrieval means no eligible evidence was returned, not proof a proposition is false.
